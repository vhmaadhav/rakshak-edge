## 4. Gaps and what Maadhav must do

1. **Campaign-disjoint SMS data is missing.** The only source with campaigns and timestamps is the IEEE S&P 2024 set. Email the authors (anahape@ncsu.edu, bgreaves@ncsu.edu) for the data. Until then, proxy clusters (text template, named entity, URL domain) are needed. The IMC25 set and PhreshPhish's `target` field are the closest substitutes.
2. **Hinglish and Hindi are thin and of mixed provenance.** Options: accept the auto-gate on `M-Arjun/SpamShield-Datasets` (shares contact info; largest multilingual set), buy or request the IEEE DataPort Hindi set (subscription), or treat `sidzzz07/scamshield-dataset` as synthetic-heavy augmentation. `bolewara` should not be counted as extra data (it mostly duplicates UCI).
3. **PhreshPhish full data is too large** (~145 GB train, ~48 GB test). Only one 1,000-row shard per split is downloaded. Decide whether to stream the test split or fetch selected shards (budget is ~2 GB).
4. **Licences to confirm:** LegitPhish (Mendeley licence null), Mishra/Soni (null), Smishtank (aggregator says CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), OpenPhish community feed (non-commercial per secondary sources; check openphish.com), PhishTank (terms unverified; check the developer page before use).
5. **PhishTank key:** the anonymous download works now. Regular refresh needs an app key, and registration reportedly closed (unverified).
6. **Smishtank:** check smishtank.com for a download page. No direct file was found.
7. **Overlap and contamination:** UCI appears in `scamshield`, `bolewara`, and Mishra/Soni (~4,753 common rows per a review). Deduplicate before any train/test split.
8. **IMC25 count mismatch:** the paper quotes ~64.5k reports, but the released CSV has 33,869 rows. Confirm which subset is the labelled dataset.
